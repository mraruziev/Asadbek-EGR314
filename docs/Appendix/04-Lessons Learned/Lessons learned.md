### Overview


## Module success 
Our project was successful with most of our planned things. We were able to move wheels, 3DoF front arm, get temperature nd humidity readings as well as the metal detector. While there were some issues during daisy chain, our project worked most of the time. 


## Lessons learned

1. I learned that UART TX0 and RX0 are not the best pins for the UARt conenction and this caused many issues with my motor's movement as my motors move freely without the daisy chain

2. I learned a lot about the importance of showing up to classes and team meetings and being able to take notes. 

3. Starting to work and double and tripple checking PCB was very important for me but I started working late so it is a lesson for me for future projects.
